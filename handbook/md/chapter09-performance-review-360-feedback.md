# Michael’s Tech Workplace English Handbook

Chapter 9: Performance Review / 360 Feedback

范围：基于你在真实工作场景中围绕 performance review、360 feedback、probation review、self-evaluation、recognition、development advice 等场景的高频英文沟通整理。

### 1. 邀请同事写 360 feedback

中文意图：礼貌邀请合作同事为自己提供反馈。

推荐英文表达：Would you be open to providing feedback for me?

更自然版本：Would you be okay to give me feedback?

更正式版本：I was wondering if you’d be comfortable providing feedback for me.

可替换模板：Would you be open to providing feedback for me on {scope}?

示例段落：Example: Since we’ve collaborated quite a bit on Data Exchange, would you be open to providing feedback for me?

常见错误替换：中式：Can you write feedback for me?
更自然：Would you be open to providing feedback for me?

### 2. 说明为什么邀请对方

中文意图：解释为什么找这个人写 feedback。

推荐英文表达：Since we’ve worked closely together on Data Exchange, I thought it would make sense to ask you for feedback.

更自然版本：Since we worked closely together, I thought I’d ask you for feedback.

更正式版本：Since we’ve collaborated closely on Data Exchange, I thought it would make sense to reach out to you for feedback.

可替换模板：Since we’ve worked closely together on {project}, I thought it would make sense to ask you for feedback.

示例段落：Example: Since we’ve worked closely together on EU–CN Data Exchange, I thought it would make sense to ask you for feedback.

常见错误替换：中式：Because we worked together, I ask you.
更自然：Since we’ve worked closely together..., I thought it would make sense to ask you for feedback.

### 3. 说明流程：HR 会发邮件

中文意图：告诉对方不需要现在写。

推荐英文表达：If you’re okay with it, the Shenzhen team will send you an email to collect the feedback.

更自然版本：If that’s okay, the Shenzhen team will send you a short email for it.

更正式版本：If you’re okay with it, the Shenzhen team will send you an email to collect the feedback formally.

可替换模板：If you’re okay with it, {team} will send you an email to collect the feedback.

示例段落：Example: If you’re okay with it, the Shenzhen HR team will send you an email to collect the feedback.

常见错误替换：中式：HR will send email to you.
更自然：The Shenzhen team will send you an email to collect the feedback.

### 4. 回复别人邀请你写 feedback

中文意图：答应帮别人写 360。

推荐英文表达：Of course — happy to help.

更自然版本：Sure, happy to help!

更正式版本：Of course, I’d be happy to help.

可替换模板：Of course — happy to help with {feedback / review}.

示例段落：Example: Of course — happy to help. Just let me know what I need to do.

常见错误替换：中式：Yes I can.
更自然：Of course — happy to help.

### 5. 询问 feedback 截止日期

中文意图：怕错过时间。

推荐英文表达：Do you know when the deadline for submitting the feedback is?

更自然版本：Do you know when the feedback deadline is?

更正式版本：Could you please let me know when the deadline for submitting the feedback is?

可替换模板：Do you know when the deadline for {feedback} is?

示例段落：Example: By the way, do you know when the deadline for submitting the feedback is?

常见错误替换：中式：When is the deadline?
更自然：Do you know when the deadline for submitting the feedback is?

### 6. 向 lead 询问反馈

中文意图：想主动了解 manager 评价。

推荐英文表达：I’d appreciate any feedback on how I can improve further.

更自然版本：Would love to hear any feedback on where I can improve.

更正式版本：I’d really appreciate any feedback on where I could improve further.

可替换模板：I’d appreciate any feedback on {improvement topic}.

示例段落：Example: I’d appreciate any feedback on how I can improve further in cross-team collaboration.

常见错误替换：中式：Please give me feedback.
更自然：I’d appreciate any feedback on how I can improve further.

### 7. 写 accomplishments 开头

### 26. 写 feedback 时保持平衡

中文意图：既讲成绩也给建议。

推荐英文表达：Overall, he has made a strong impact in this area, and I believe he can increase that impact even further by focusing on process stability and documentation.

更自然版本：Overall, he’s already made a strong impact, and I think he can go even further by focusing on stability and documentation.

更正式版本：Overall, he has made a strong impact in this area, and I believe he can further increase that impact by focusing on process stability and documentation.

可替换模板：Overall, {person} has made a strong impact in {area}, and I believe {future potential}.

示例段落：Example: Overall, he has made a strong impact in this area, and I believe he can increase that impact even further by focusing on process stability and documentation.

常见错误替换：中式：He is good but can improve.
更自然：Overall, he has made a strong impact..., and I believe he can increase that impact even further by...

### 3 个完整 feedback 示例

#### Example 1：邀请 Paolo 给我写 360 feedback

Hi Paolo,

The Performance Evaluation 2025 for the Shenzhen site has started, and each employee is encouraged to ask colleagues they’ve worked with for some feedback.

Since I’m responsible for the CN data center and we’ve collaborated quite a bit on Data Exchange, I was wondering if you’d be comfortable providing feedback for me.

If you’re okay with it, the Shenzhen team will send you an email to collect the feedback.

Thanks a lot in advance — I really appreciate it!

#### Example 2：给 Paolo 写 accomplishments + strengths + advice

Paolo has played a key role in facilitating smooth collaboration between the CN and EU teams in the Data Exchange area. He has been very supportive in clarifying data structures, policies, and constraints, which helped us better understand the EU data landscape and align our implementation accordingly.

With his support, multiple product-related datasets were successfully transferred to CN and became valuable inputs for many downstream projects. One of his key strengths is his ability to collaborate effectively across teams and regions. He communicates clearly and makes complex topics easier to align on.

One area that could further increase his impact is improving the stability and predictability of the data exchange process, especially for downstream consumers.

#### Example 3：probation review next steps

Going forward, I’d like to focus more on prioritizing the highest-impact business scenarios and aligning them with technical execution. Another priority is to improve ROI by reducing unnecessary storage and processing costs.

I’d also like to define clearer phased goals for the next stage of the work, so that delivery and impact are easier to track across the different projects.
