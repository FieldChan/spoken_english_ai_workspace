# Michael’s Tech Workplace English Handbook

Chapter 1: Daily Communication

范围：基于你在真实工作场景中出现过的日常沟通表达，聚焦 Data Exchange、Data Pipeline、GitHub、Weekly Sync、EU-CN 协作等高频场景。

### 1. 跟进进度：礼貌询问有没有新进展

中文意图：不催人地跟进某个 issue、数据集、legal review、pipeline 或 PR 的进展。

推荐英文表达：Just wanted to follow up on this. Have you had a chance to take a look?

Google Chat 口语版：Just checking in — any updates on this?

Email 正式版：I just wanted to check whether there have been any updates on this.

适用场景：Google Chat / Slack 跟进 issue、数据交换进度、schema review、pipeline 状态。

可替换句型模板：Just wanted to follow up on {topic}. Have you had a chance to {action}?

Michael 常见中式英语替换：中式：Is there any update?
更自然：Just checking in — any updates on this?

### 2. 温和提醒：提醒别人看一下

中文意图：提醒对方查看消息、文档或请求，不显得 push。

推荐英文表达：Just a gentle reminder on this when you have a chance.

Google Chat 口语版：Just a quick reminder on this 🙂

Email 正式版：Just a gentle reminder on this whenever you have a moment.

适用场景：对方几天未回复；需要对方 review 文档、回复群消息、确认需求。

可替换句型模板：Just a gentle reminder on {topic} when you have a chance.

Michael 常见中式英语替换：中式：Please check this.
更自然：Just a gentle reminder on this when you have a chance.

可替换句型模板：Got it, thanks for clarifying {point}.

Michael 常见中式英语替换：中式：thanks for clarify
更自然：Thanks for clarifying.
