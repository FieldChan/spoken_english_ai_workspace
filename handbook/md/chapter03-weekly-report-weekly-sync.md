# Michael’s Tech Workplace English Handbook

Chapter 3: Weekly Report & Weekly Sync

范围：基于你在真实工作场景中写英文周报、做 weekly sync 口头汇报时反复使用过的表达，聚焦 Data Exchange、Data Processing、Pipeline、Legal Review、Blocker / Risk 等高频场景。

### 1. 周报总起句：本周有几个更新

中文意图：开始 weekly sync / stand-up 时自然开场。

书面周报表达：This week I have a few updates.

口述版表达：So for this week, I have a few updates.

可替换模板：This week I have a few updates on {topic}.

示例句：This week I have a few updates on Data Exchange and Multimodal Understanding.

常见错误替换：中式：This week I want to report...
更自然：This week I have a few updates.

### 2. Completed：已完成某项工作

中文意图：在周报中表达某项任务已完成。

书面周报表达：Completed development of the Semantic Tag Processor.

口述版表达：We finished the Semantic Tag Processor this week.

可替换模板：Completed development / integration / setup of {task}.

示例句：Completed development of the Statistical Tag Processor.

常见错误替换：中式：We already done...
更自然：Completed development of...

### 3. Successfully obtained / received

中文意图：强调某个数据集或资源已经成功拿到。

书面周报表达：Successfully obtained the search data with the new country field.

口述版表达：We successfully received the search data with the new country field.

可替换模板：Successfully obtained / received {dataset}.

示例句：Successfully received and integrated the Product Availability dataset.

常见错误替换：中式：Successfully got...
更自然：Successfully obtained / received...

### 4. 已完成集成

中文意图：表达数据集已进入 Data Center / Data Warehouse。

书面周报表达：The dataset has been integrated into the Data Center.

口述版表达：The dataset is already in our Data Center now.

可替换模板：{dataset} has been integrated into {system}.

示例句：The Product Categories dataset has been integrated into the Data Warehouse.

常见错误替换：中式：The dataset has integrated into...
更自然：The dataset has been integrated into...

### 5. In progress：正在推进

中文意图：表达某项工作还在进行中。

书面周报表达：Development of the Concept Tag Processor is still in progress.

口述版表达：We’re still working on the Concept Tag Processor.

可替换模板：{task} is still in progress.

示例句：Legal review is still in progress.

常见错误替换：中式：We are doing...
更自然：...is still in progress / We’re still working on...

### 6. 当前正在做什么

中文意图：描述进行中的关键任务。

书面周报表达：We are currently performing data exploration and validation.

口述版表达：Right now we’re exploring the data and checking whether it meets our needs.

可替换模板：We are currently {verb-ing} {task}.

示例句：We are currently migrating the semantic and analysis tasks to Databricks.

常见错误替换：中式：Now we do...
更自然：We are currently...

### 7. Next week plan

中文意图：说明下周计划。

书面周报表达：Plan for next week: deploy the full pipeline to the London environment.

口述版表达：Next week, we plan to deploy the full pipeline to the London environment.

可替换模板：Plan for next week: {action}.

示例句：Plan for next week: complete end-to-end testing in Frankfurt.

常见错误替换：中式：Next week we will maybe...
更自然：Next week, we plan to...

### 8. Data Exchange 周报写法

中文意图：报告数据交换进展。

书面周报表达：The DPR has been submitted for Legal review.

口述版表达：The DPR is already with Legal now.

可替换模板：The DPR has been submitted for Legal review.

示例句：The EU data team has updated the DPR and reached out to Legal.

常见错误替换：中式：We sent DPR to legal.
更自然：The DPR has been submitted for Legal review.

### 9. Legal review 进展

中文意图：说明法务进度。

书面周报表达：Currently in the legal review stage.

口述版表达：It’s currently with Legal for review.

可替换模板：Currently in the legal review stage.

示例句：The request is currently in the legal review stage.

常见错误替换：中式：Legal is reviewing it now.
更自然：It’s currently with Legal for review.

### 10. Pipeline 部署进展

中文意图：说明部署状态。

书面周报表达：We are preparing to deploy the full data pipeline to the Frankfurt environment.

口述版表达：We’re now preparing the Frankfurt deployment.

可替换模板：We are preparing to deploy {pipeline} to {environment}.

示例句：We are preparing to deploy the multimodal pipeline to Frankfurt.

常见错误替换：中式：We will deploy pipeline to...
更自然：We are preparing to deploy...

### 11. 等待审批

中文意图：说明被卡在审批流程。

书面周报表达：We are currently waiting for the approval of the PCM request.

口述版表达：At the moment, we’re waiting for the PCM request to be approved.

可替换模板：We are currently waiting for the approval of {request}.

示例句：We are currently waiting for the approval of the security ticket.

常见错误替换：中式：We are waiting approval.
更自然：We are currently waiting for the approval of...

### 12. 风险 / Blocker 表达

中文意图：说明风险存在，但不夸张。

书面周报表达：One current blocker is the approval of the PCM request.

口述版表达：The main blocker right now is the PCM approval.

可替换模板：One current blocker is {issue}.

示例句：One current blocker is the pipeline setup in Frankfurt.

常见错误替换：中式：The problem is...
更自然：One current blocker is...

### 13. 表达担忧

中文意图：委婉指出潜在风险。

书面周报表达：I’m a bit concerned that this could affect data quality in the longer term.

口述版表达：I’m a bit worried this may affect data quality later on.

可替换模板：I’m a bit concerned that {issue} could affect {impact}.

示例句：I’m a bit concerned that missing one week of data could affect trend detection.

常见错误替换：中式：This is very bad.
更自然：I’m a bit concerned that...

### 14. 表达目前不阻塞

中文意图：说明暂时不影响，但值得关注。

书面周报表达：This is not currently blocking us, but it is something we should keep an eye on.

口述版表达：It’s not blocking us now, but we should still watch it.

可替换模板：This is not currently blocking us, but {concern}.

示例句：This is not currently blocking us, but it may impact data quality later.

常见错误替换：中式：Now no issue.
更自然：This is not currently blocking us...

### 15. Thank someone in weekly report

中文意图：在周报里感谢同事支持。

书面周报表达：Special thanks to Paolo for the support on the legal coordination.

口述版表达：Big thanks to Paolo for helping push the legal part forward.

可替换模板：Special thanks to {name} for {support}.

示例句：Special thanks to Bin for the support on legal coordination.

常见错误替换：中式：Thanks Bin help us.
更自然：Special thanks to Bin for the support on...

### 16. 汇报多个 completed 项

中文意图：一段里写多个完成事项。

书面周报表达：Completed the Semantic Tag Processor and the Statistical Tag Processor.

口述版表达：We finished both the Semantic Tag Processor and the Statistical Tag Processor this week.

可替换模板：Completed {task1} and {task2}.

示例句：Completed the dashboard review and the schema adjustment.

常见错误替换：中式：Finished A, finished B.
更自然：Completed A and B.

### 17. 表达数据探查中

中文意图：还在判断数据是否符合需求。

书面周报表达：We are currently exploring the data to assess whether it can fully support our business needs.

口述版表达：We’re still looking into the data to see whether it meets our needs.

可替换模板：We are currently exploring the data to assess whether it can support {need}.

示例句：We are currently exploring the Trend Spotter data to assess whether it is sufficient.

常见错误替换：中式：We are checking if data is enough.
更自然：We are currently exploring the data to assess whether...

### 18. 表达 schema 变更影响

中文意图：说明由于 schema 调整，工作在进行中。

书面周报表达：Due to schema changes, the data pipeline is currently being adjusted.

口述版表达：The pipeline is being adjusted because of the schema change.

可替换模板：Due to {reason}, {task} is currently being adjusted.

示例句：Due to schema changes, the weekly and monthly exports are still being updated.

常见错误替换：中式：Because schema changed, we are changing pipeline.
更自然：Due to schema changes, the pipeline is currently being adjusted.

### 19. Dry-run 数据

中文意图：表达拿到了 dry run 数据。

书面周报表达：We received the dry-run datasets for Oct 6 and Oct 8.

口述版表达：We already got the dry-run data for Oct 6 and Oct 8.

可替换模板：We received the dry-run datasets for {date1} and {date2}.

示例句：We received the dry-run data for the first two livestream dates.

常见错误替换：中式：We got test data.
更自然：We received the dry-run datasets...

### 20. 口头汇报转场

中文意图：周会上从一个模块切到另一个模块。

书面周报表达：On the data processing side, ...

口述版表达：On the data processing side, ...

可替换模板：On the {module} side, ...

示例句：On the data exchange side, we have two updates this week.

常见错误替换：中式：For data processing...
更自然：On the data processing side...

书面周报表达：Bring the London pipelines to a production-ready state.

### 23. 说明迁移计划

中文意图：表达系统迁移或环境迁移。

书面周报表达：We are planning to migrate the jobs from London EMR to Databricks.

口述版表达：We’re moving the jobs from EMR to Databricks.

可替换模板：We are planning to migrate {jobs/system} from {A} to {B}.

示例句：We are planning to migrate the semantic jobs from London EMR to Databricks.

常见错误替换：中式：We will move jobs to...
更自然：We are planning to migrate...

### 24. 用 richer / more accurate 描述数据改进

中文意图：说明为什么替换旧数据集。

书面周报表达：We found that the SPP dataset contains richer and more accurate product information.

口述版表达：We realized that SPP has much richer and more accurate product information.

可替换模板：We found that {dataset} contains richer and more accurate {information}.

示例句：We found that the new curated data contains richer brand and category information.

常见错误替换：中式：This dataset has more information.
更自然：This dataset contains richer and more accurate information.

### 25. 说明替换旧数据集

中文意图：表达为了提升效果替换数据源。

书面周报表达：We are replacing the original product_textual dataset with SPP to improve tagging accuracy.

口述版表达：We’re switching from product_textual to SPP to improve the tagging quality.

可替换模板：We are replacing {old} with {new} to improve {goal}.

示例句：We are replacing the old image source with packshots to reduce PII risk.

常见错误替换：中式：Use new dataset instead old dataset.
更自然：We are replacing the original dataset with...

### 5 个完整周报模板

#### Template 1：Data Exchange Weekly Update

This week on Data Exchange:
- Successfully received and integrated the Product Availability dataset.
- The DPR for the ZA metrics request has been submitted and is now with Legal.
- We are currently waiting for schema confirmation for the new Trend Spotter datasets.
- Next week, we plan to continue the data exploration and confirm the fields needed for downstream use cases.

#### Template 2：Data Processing Weekly Update

This week on Data Processing:
- Completed development of the Semantic Tag Processor and the Statistical Tag Processor.
- Development of the Concept Tag Processor is still in progress.
- We are currently validating the generated tag data and checking the logic.
- Next week, we plan to complete the Concept Tag Processor and start end-to-end testing.

#### Template 3：Pipeline / Deployment Weekly Update

This week on deployment:
- We aligned with the EU team on the Frankfurt deployment approach.
- We completed the planning work for migrating jobs from London EMR to Databricks.
- We are currently waiting for PCM approval before proceeding with the Frankfurt deployment.
- Next week, we plan to push the code to the Frankfurt repos and continue deployment setup.

#### Template 4：Legal Review Weekly Update

This week on Legal / Compliance:
- The DPR has been submitted for Legal review.
- We are currently in the legal review stage.
- One open question is whether the image URL column needs to be dropped while keeping object_key-related metadata.
- Next week, we hope to get further feedback from Legal and move the request forward.

#### Template 5：Mixed Weekly Sync (spoken style)

So for this week, I have three updates.
First, on Data Exchange, we successfully received the Product Availability dataset and started exploring the data.
Second, on Data Processing, we completed the Semantic and Statistical Tag Processors, and we’re still working on Concept Tagging.
Third, on deployment, we aligned with the EU team on the Frankfurt setup and are now waiting for PCM approval.
That’s all from my side for this week.
