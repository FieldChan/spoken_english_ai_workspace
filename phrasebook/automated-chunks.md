# Automated Chunks

The graduation record for Track A. A chunk is only listed under `## Graduated` once it is genuinely a reflex, not once it has been practiced.

## Graduation Criteria

Both conditions:

1. Said in **≤5 seconds** from a Chinese prompt, on **3 different dates**
2. Used at least once in **real work** — a real message sent or a real sentence spoken to a colleague

When a chunk graduates, promote the next chunk from the Priority Ladder in `training/english-improvement-plan.md` into the core. The core always holds exactly ten.

A chunk returns to the core if it shows up as a mistake again.
