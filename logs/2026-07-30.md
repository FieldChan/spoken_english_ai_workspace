Original:

> Could you please help check if this job has been finished?

Better:

> Could you please help check whether this job has completed successfully?

Result: Passed. The structure `help + base verb + whether` was correct in both the review and the final simulation. Correct streak: 1/3.

## Self-Correction

- Use singular `Brief update` for one status message.
- Say `in progress`, not `on progress`.
- Prefer `has been completed` for a completed check and `has completed successfully` for a successful job.
- Use an adverb with a verb: `completed successfully`, not `successful finished`.
- Keep the explicit risk bridge: `There are no blockers at the moment, but...`.
- Spelling: `brief`, `confirm`, `affected`, and `tomorrow`.
