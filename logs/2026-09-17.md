### Correction Points

1. Use `To avoid impacting this week's release`, not `to not influence this week's public`.
2. Specify the owner: `confirm who owns the approval`.
3. Use `expected timeline`, not an unfinished approval phrase.
4. Complete the offer: `If needed, I can provide the required materials.`
