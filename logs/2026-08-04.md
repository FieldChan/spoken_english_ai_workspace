## Self-Correction

- Use `in charge of`, not `on charge of`.
- Say `run the validation and summarize the results`, not `prepare for the result`.
- Use `by Wednesday` for a deadline.
- Say `Legal approval is delayed`, not `Legal approval is late`.
- Use `responsibilities` or `division of responsibilities`, not `job distribution`.
- Prefer `Please let me know`, not `Please tell me`.
- Use `missing some context`, not `lack of context` as a verb.
- Use past tense for a past misunderstanding: `I misunderstood it`.
- Prefer `full pipeline`, `end-to-end testing`, and `obtain security approval`.
