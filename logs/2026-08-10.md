## Self-Correction

- Use `The original plan was to...` or `We originally planned to...`.
- Use `by Wednesday` for a deadline and `pushed back to Friday` for a delay.
- After `has not`, use the past participle: `has not confirmed`.
- Say `To reduce/minimize the impact`, not `For reduce the influence`.
- Use `run/perform the validation`, not `execute the validation` in normal workplace speech.
- Use `risk` for a possible future problem: `if any new risks arise`.
- Prefer `missing some context` to `lack some background`.
- Use `migration planning` and `continue preparing for deployment`.
- Spelling: `Wednesday`, `will`, and `migration`.
