More formal version:

> We submitted an access request last week and would like to check on the expected processing timeline. There is no urgency; we would simply like to stay aligned. Could you please advise which role we should apply for and whom we should contact? In addition, could you please share the schema documentation? I will be supporting this data exchange, so please keep me posted on any progress.

## Self-Correction

Key improvements:

- Use `expected timeline` or `expected processing time`, not `expectation timeline`.
- Use `I just wanted to...` to make a gentle follow-up sound complete.
- Use direct-question order: `Which role should we apply for?`
- Use `I'll be helping with...` or `I'll help with...`, not `I will helping handle...`.
- Split the final support statement and update request into two short sentences.
